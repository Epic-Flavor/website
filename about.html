Meet Chef Slade

Epic Flavor is the result of Chef William Slade’s forty years in hospitality and his love of cooking.

He grew up in Beirut, Lebanon, the son of professors, and was exposed early to the food of many cultures. He spent fourteen years training under Hungarian Master Chef Bank Szerenyi, and has since served as head chef at several well-known restaurants and catering companies, including as Director of Food and Beverage at The Founders Inn and Spa.

Today he cooks as a personal chef and caterer, building custom menus around each client’s tastes, dietary needs and preferences.
